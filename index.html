About me

I am a final-year Ph.D. student in Biostatistics at Duke-NUS Medical School, supervised of Prof. Prof. Bibhas Chakraborty, with an expected graduation in July 2025. Previously, I earned my Master's degree in Biostatistics and a dual Bachelor's degree in Medicine and Applied Mathematics from Peking University.

Research Interest

I develop statistical and machine learning methodologies for impactful applications in digital health. My primary research interests include:

  • Sequential decision making for adaptive interventions

  • Contextual bandits and Reinforcement learning

  • Longitudinal data analysis and Causal Inference

  • Online and Adaptive experiment design
