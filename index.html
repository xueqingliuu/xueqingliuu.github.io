About me

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Statistics at Harvard University, working with Professor Susan Murphy. I received my Ph.D. in Biostatistics and Health Data Science from the Duke-NUS Medical School, where I was advised by Professor Bibhas Chakraborty. Earlier, I earned my Master's degree and Bachelor's degree from Peking University.

Research Interest

I develop statistical and machine learning methods for impactful applications in digital health. My primary research interests include:

  • Contextual bandits and Reinforcement learning

  • Longitudinal data analysis and Causal Inference

  • Design and analysis of experiments

  • Just-in-time adaptive intervention
