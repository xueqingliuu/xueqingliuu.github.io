Recommended reading

  1. High dimension

    1. Martin Wainwright, High-Dimensional Statistics: A Non-Asymptotic Viewpoint.

    2. Philippe Rigollet and Jan-Christian Huetter, High Dimensional Statistics. Lecture Notes

    3. Roman Vershynin, High-Dimensional Probability: An Introduction with Applications in Data Science,[notes]

    4. Ramon van Handel, Probability in High Dimension

  2. Random matrix

    1. Terence Tao, Topics in random matrix theory

    2. Jack William Silverstein and Zhidong Bai, Spectral Analysis of Large Dimensional Random Matrices

  3. Nonparametric

    1. Alexandre B. Tsybakov, Introduction to Nonparametric Estimation notes

    2. Iain M. Johnstone, Gaussian estimation: Sequence and wavelet models

  4. Asymptotic

    1. Aad van der Vaart, Asymptotic Statistics
